QUESTIONS DEALERS ACTUALLY ASK

Common questions.
Honest answers.

I'm a dealer principal with multiple stores. Does this work for groups?
Yes. DeskView is built so dealer groups can monitor every rooftop from one mobile view — combined pace, side-by-side store comparison, and drill-down into any rooftop, manager, or rep. Group-level visibility is on the roadmap with founding-dealer groups shaping what we build first. Get in touch if you run 2+ stores.
How is this different from the daily reports my GM sends me?
Your daily reports tell you what already happened. By the time you read them, you can't act on them. DeskView shows you what's happening right now — store-wide pace, who's tracking, who's slipping, deals as they close. Plus an auto-generated AI briefing every morning that summarizes the day with the same intelligence a great GM would deliver — without waiting for your GM to put it together. You see what your GM sees, when they see it.
Does DeskView AI integrate with my DMS?
DeskView AI sits alongside your DMS, not on top of it. Your DMS handles accounting, compliance, and end-of-month reporting. DeskView AI handles real-time floor intelligence — UPs, sold notifications, salesperson pacing, AI coaching. Direct DMS integrations are on the roadmap based on customer demand.
How is this different from my CRM?
A CRM tracks customer conversations and follow-ups. DeskView AI tracks what's happening on your sales floor right now: who took the last UP, who needs coaching, which deals are stuck, who's on pace. Most dealers find DeskView complements their CRM rather than replacing it — your CRM still owns customer records and outreach; DeskView owns floor operations and management intelligence.
How long does setup take?
Days, not months. No consultants required. You give us your sales team list and monthly unit goals, and your team starts using DeskView AI the same day. Real-time data flows once your team logs their first few deals — typically within 24-48 hours of go-live.
What does pricing look like?
Flat per-store monthly pricing — no per-user fees, no per-deal charges, no annual contracts. Pricing scales with store size so you only pay for what fits. See the full breakdown on the pricing page or email sales@deskview.ai for a quote tailored to your dealership.
Can I try it before committing?
Yes — every evaluation starts with a 20-minute personalized demo where we walk you through DeskView AI with data shaped like your dealership. After that, qualifying stores can run a 30-day pilot with real deals and your team. Month-to-month means you're never locked in.
What's the Founding Dealer Program?
We're partnering with a limited cohort of dealerships to refine DeskView AI alongside the floor managers and salespeople who actually use it. Founding dealers get lifetime pricing lock (their rate never goes up), direct access to the founder (no support ticket queue), and roadmap input — including which DMS integrations we build first. Cohort is intentionally small. Get in touch to discuss whether your store is a fit.
Do my salespeople need training to use it?
No formal training needed. DeskView AI is built for the floor — large tap targets, mobile-first, no menus to learn. Most salespeople pick it up in their first day. The AI Game Plans tell each rep what to do next; the live UP queue tells them whose turn it is. If they can use a smartphone, they can use DeskView AI.
Will it work on iPhones, iPads, and Android phones?
Yes. DeskView AI is mobile-first and installs as a native-feeling app on iPhones, iPads, and Android devices via Progressive Web App (PWA) technology. Your team uses their existing devices — no hardware to buy, no app store approval to wait for.
Is my dealership data secure?
Yes. We use industry-standard encryption (TLS 1.3 in transit, encryption at rest), authentication on every request, and row-level security so each dealership only accesses its own data. We don't sell your data, share it with advertisers, or train third-party AI models on your dealership data. Full details in our Privacy Policy.
